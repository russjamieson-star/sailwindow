# SailWindow Morning Brief — Monday, September 28, 2026

**Coffee check:** Today looks promising for short bay sails. Texas has the clearest building-breeze opportunities; much of the central Gulf and Florida is light-air territory. Miami’s quiet wind does not mean the offshore swell has disappeared.

**Timing note:** Windows below are interpretations of broad NWS forecast periods, not verified hourly departure slots. Use local time: CDT in Texas/LA/MS/AL/western Panhandle; EDT on Florida’s peninsula and in the Keys. Sources checked this morning; older forecasts are marked provisional.

It identifies no Gulf/Florida development area; that is not a local all-clear. [NHC outlook](https://tgftp.nws.noaa.gov/data/raw/ab/abnt20.knhc.two.at.txt)
- **Today’s limits:** Check radar and current warnings before leaving. Squalls can overwhelm the light background wind; check inlet conditions separately from protected-bay forecasts. [NWS marine warnings and forecasts](https://www.weather.gov/marine/)
- **Later-week change:** Texas and central Gulf forecasts strengthen into midweek; Florida also trends windier and wetter. Treat today’s easy bay conditions as a day-sail opportunity, not permission for an extended passage without another forecast check. [Corpus forecast](https://tgftp.nws.noaa.gov/data/raw/fz/fzus54.kcrp.cwf.crp.txt), [Mobile forecast](https://tgftp.nws.noaa.gov/data/raw/fz/fzus54.kmob.cwf.mob.txt), [Keys forecast](https://tgftp.nws.noaa.gov/data/raw/fz/fzus52.kkey.cwf.key.txt)
