- **Granting lifetime access when someone hits 5:** Stripe Dashboard → Customers → find the brother → his subscription → **Apply coupon** → **FRIENDS-100** (100% off forever). This replaces the 4-month coupon, so he's never charged.
- **If they don't hit 5:** nothing to do — the subscription continues at $5.99/month after the 4 free months, as the email explains.
