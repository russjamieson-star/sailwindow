**Tides and watch-outs:** Surge and swell matter more than an ordinary tide-table “sweet spot” in affected areas. NHC forecasts possible 5–7 feet of inundation above ground for Ocean Springs–Indian Pass and Mobile Bay if peak surge coincides with high tide; that is not a tide height or channel-depth forecast. Exact local high/low times were not verified. Consult [NOAA observed water levels/inundation](https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/inundationdb/) and local warnings; astronomical predictions alone cannot describe storm water levels. Check marina access and official port/bridge restrictions directly. Recheck radar, warnings, and your return route before casting off.
